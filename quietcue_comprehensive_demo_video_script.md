## Full Presentation Script

### 0:00 – 0:25 | The Inspiration & Core Concept
- Visual: Master console overview. Focus on the clean QuietCue+ interface and active dashboard header.
- Voiceover:
  > The idea for QuietCue+ started on my couch a few weeks ago while watching television. A banner popped up across the screen for a routine smart home update: my thermostat had switched to eco mode. It was helpful information, but it did not need to interrupt the scene. That could easily wait for a natural break. At the same time, if a delivery arrives at my front door, I cannot keep a parcel waiting outside. I need to know right away, but without a heavy banner blocking the video. A gentle on-screen whisper lets me know immediately so I can grab it without disrupting the room. And if there is a safety alert, that needs an instant chime. Alerts need genuine context intelligence. That insight led me to build QuietCue+ for Fire TV and Alexa+.

---

### 0:25 – 0:55 | Alexa+ Session Contracts via Amazon Bedrock
- Visual: Type or click a contract prompt in the conversation panel. Show the assistant parsing the policy and activating the contract.
- Voiceover:
  > Instead of digging through settings, you just tell Alexa what you are doing, powered by Amazon Bedrock. Here I say: "I am starting a session. Only interrupt me for critical alerts, show standard updates on a break, and summarize routine updates afterward."
  > 
  > QuietCue+ instantly sets three simple rules:
  > - Priority 1 safety alerts come through right away with two beeps.
  > - Priority 2 standard updates wait quietly until I pause.
  > - Priority 3 to 5 routine updates are saved for a summary at the end.

---

### 0:55 – 1:25 | Fire TV Client & Real-Time On-Screen HUD
- Visual: Focus on the TV screen display and live video stream. Trigger an alert to display the top HUD.
- Voiceover:
  > I built this Fire TV Client simulator to demonstrate how notifications feel on an active television screen.
  > 
  > When an alert comes in, a lightweight on-screen HUD appears at the top with a soft chime so it never obstructs the video plane. Right below the stream, the playback controls and the three alert rules stay visible so you can easily see what is being held back.

---

### 1:25 – 1:55 | Alexa Voice Calling, Auto-Typing & Lifestyle Routines
- Visual: Click an Alexa voice chip or lifestyle routine. Show the animated overlay auto-typing text character by character across the television screen.
- Voiceover:
  > To make interaction completely hands-free, I integrated Alexa voice calling and live auto-typing. When I call out a stream or lifestyle routine, Alexa auto-types the text live across the television screen character by character with an active cursor.
  > 
  > Whether I activate a Morning Lifestyle Routine, a Hydration Reset, or a Bedtime Quiet Routine, QuietCue+ automatically adjusts notification flow to match my daily rhythm without touching a remote.

---

  > 
  > QuietCue+ also switches instantly across six languages, including Hindi and Japanese, ensuring universal clarity wherever it is deployed.

### 2:20 – 2:40 | Conclusion & Impact
- Visual: Camera pans across the full dashboard showing active contract, connected devices, and audio mixer.
- Voiceover:
  > Smart technology should adapt to what you are doing, wherever you are. QuietCue+ lets you stay focused or relaxed without missing what matters. It is a smarter notification system that knows when to speak, when to whisper, and when to wait.
